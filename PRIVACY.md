# Privacy Policy

BluetoothBatteryWatch doesn't collect, store or send any personal data.

- **Battery levels and device names** are read from macOS on your Mac. They're only shown in the menu and in the app's notifications, and they never leave your Mac.
- **Settings** (your warning threshold and which devices you've already been warned about) are stored locally in the app's preferences.
- **No network access.** The app doesn't connect to the internet. It contains no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no third-party code.
- **Bluetooth.** The app uses Bluetooth access only to look up the names of accessories that are already connected to your Mac.

Deleting the app and its preferences removes everything it stored.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/bombadiltom/BluetoothBatteryWatch/issues>.
